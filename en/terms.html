02 The service

ShiftPro is an app for managing work shifts, rotations and a pay estimate. It comes in two tiers:

  • Free — shifts, rotations, calendar, automatic overtime calculation, a gross estimate with all premiums, your country's public holidays, an account with sync across devices: no cost.
  • ShiftPro Premium — a net estimate for the month and the year, advanced statistics (year-to-date summary and trend, month compared, weekly average, awkward shifts, how the month ends), export of the month and the year as PDF and of the month's shifts as an image, export to external calendars: monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-off lifetime purchase — the exact price is shown in the app at the time of purchase. Where a free trial is offered, its length is shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. The free trial is an App Store offer, available only once per Apple Account: if you don't cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription starts at the price shown.

If you were already using ShiftPro before version 1.1, the net estimate stays free for 30 days from the first time you open this version on each of your devices: the app shows you the exact date while the period is active. After that, net pay is part of ShiftPro Premium like for everyone else.

Estimates, not advice. The figures in ShiftPro (gross, net, overtime, public holidays, rest hours) are calculated from the parameters you enter and from general rules that may not match your contract, your collective agreement or rules that changed recently. They are an estimate: they don't replace your payslip, have no evidential value towards your employer or third parties, and are not tax, legal, social-security or employment advice. To check them, ask your employer, a payroll adviser, a tax advice service or a trade union.

06 ShiftPro Premium

ShiftPro Premium is available in three forms, sold through the App Store: an auto-renewing monthly subscription, an auto-renewing yearly subscription, or a one-off lifetime purchase (no renewal, access with no expiry for a single payment, for as long as ShiftPro is offered). The exact price, the currency and — for the two subscriptions — the length are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm: those values, not a figure written here, are the binding ones.

Auto-renewal (monthly and yearly only) — the subscription renews automatically at the end of each period for the same length, charged to your Apple payment method, unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The lifetime purchase doesn't renew: it's a single payment, there's nothing to cancel and Premium access stays active with no expiry for as long as ShiftPro is offered (section 10) — on your Apple Account, and on the ShiftPro account you bought it with if you were signed in (see Restore below).

How to cancel (monthly and yearly only) — you can cancel at any time from your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from ShiftPro's Settings through the direct link to Apple's subscription management. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium access stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

Restore — if you change device, the "Restore purchases" button in ShiftPro recovers a subscription that's already active or a lifetime purchase already made on your Apple Account, without paying again.

You need an account to use ShiftPro Premium — always: it's what stops anyone who picks up your phone, or uses it after you, from getting the features you pay for. For the same reason you buy while signed in. A purchase made while signed in stays tied to that ShiftPro account as well as to your Apple Account: to use it on another device, sign in with the same account. If you bought before having an account, just create one: at your first sign-in the purchase becomes yours and you don't lose it. If you delete your ShiftPro account you don't lose the purchase: on this device it becomes active again as soon as you create a new account. If you need to recover it elsewhere after deleting your account, write to matteo.de.min@outlook.it.

On a device with a different Apple Account — signing in to your ShiftPro account is enough: the plan and its expiry date are recorded on our servers, so the Premium features are there even where the App Store knows nothing about that purchase. There, however, changing plan and cancelling can't be done, because they go through Apple and Apple ties them to the Apple Account you bought with: you do them from that device, or from any device signed in with that Apple Account. On a device with a different Apple Account, Settings → your name → Subscriptions does not show that subscription: there Apple only lists the subscriptions of the Apple Account in use.
