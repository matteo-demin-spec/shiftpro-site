02 The service

ShiftPro is an app for managing work shifts, rotas and a pay estimate. It comes in two tiers:

  • Free — shifts, rotas, calendar, automatic overtime calculation, monthly pay estimate, sync across devices with an account: no cost, for ever.
  • ShiftPro Premium — advanced statistics (yearly summary, trends over time), PDF export, export to external calendars: monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-off lifetime purchase — the exact price is shown in the app at the time of purchase.
The estimates are not an official document. The pay figures you see in ShiftPro are calculated from the figures you enter (hourly rate, supplements, thresholds): they are an estimate, they don't replace your payslip and they have no evidential value towards your employer or third parties.

03 Your account

Creating an account is always optional: without one, ShiftPro still works, locally on your device. If you choose to create one:

  • You are responsible for keeping your password confidential.
  • One account can be used on several of your devices, for sync.
  • You can delete the account at any time from Settings: deletion is permanent.

04 ShiftPro Premium

ShiftPro Premium is available in three forms, sold through the App Store: an auto-renewing monthly subscription, an auto-renewing yearly subscription, or a one-off lifetime purchase (no renewal, permanent access for a single payment). The exact price, the currency and — for the two subscriptions — the length are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm: those values, not a figure written here, are the binding ones.

Auto-renewal (monthly and yearly only) — the subscription renews automatically at the end of each period for the same length, charged to your Apple payment method, unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The lifetime purchase doesn't renew: it's a single payment, there's nothing to cancel and Premium access stays active for ever — on your Apple Account, and on the ShiftPro account you bought it with if you were signed in (see Restore below).

How to cancel (monthly and yearly only) — you can cancel at any time from your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or from ShiftPro's Settings through the direct link to Apple's subscription management. Cancelling stops the next renewal; Premium access stays active until the end of the period already paid for.

Restore — if you change device, the “Restore purchases” button in ShiftPro (in the app, still in Italian: “Ripristina acquisti”) recovers a subscription that's already active or a lifetime purchase already made on your Apple Account, without paying again.

You need an account to use ShiftPro Premium — always: it's what stops anyone who picks up your phone, or uses it after you, from getting the features you pay for. For the same reason you buy while signed in: if you don't have an account yet, the purchase screen takes you to create one before paying. A purchase made while signed in stays tied to that ShiftPro account as well as to your Apple Account: to use it on another device, sign in with the same account. If you bought before having an account, just create one: at your first sign-in the purchase becomes yours and you don't lose it. If you delete your ShiftPro account you don't lose the purchase: on this device it becomes active again as soon as you create a new account. If you need to recover it elsewhere after deleting your account, write to matteo.de.min@outlook.it.

On a device with a different Apple Account — since version 1.0.8, signing in to your ShiftPro account is enough: the plan and its expiry date are recorded on our servers, so the Premium features are there even where the App Store knows nothing about that purchase. There, however, changing plan and cancelling can't be done, because they go through Apple and Apple ties them to the Apple Account you bought with: you do them from that device, or from any device signed in with that Apple Account. On a device with a different Apple Account, Settings → your name → Subscriptions does not show that subscription: there Apple only lists the subscriptions of the Apple Account in use.

05 Right of withdrawal

As a consumer in the European Union, you have the right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of its conclusion, without giving any reason.

If you believe the charge doesn't match what was described, or that your consent to immediate performance wasn't properly asked for, you're still entitled to a refund: write to us, or follow Apple's procedure in the next point.

Exception for digital content supplied immediately. ShiftPro Premium gives you access to the Premium features straight away after purchase, not after 14 days. Before you confirm, Apple's purchase sheet shows the information about the right of withdrawal: that's where you state that you want immediate access and that you know that, by doing so, you lose the right of withdrawal once supply has begun. That step belongs to Apple, which is the seller: ShiftPro doesn't collect a separate confirmation.
